Python · pandas

Product sales, 12 months

Problem
A year of raw electronics orders from 10 US cities. When and where does revenue come from, when should ads run, and what sells together?
Approach
Cleaned the order data in pandas (blank and malformed rows, types) down to 186,305 orders, derived month, city and hour columns, then grouped orders and paired products bought in the same order.
Outcome
December is the best month at $4.61M and San Francisco the biggest city. Orders peak around 12 PM and 7 PM, so ads should run just before. A phone plus its charging cable is the strongest bundle.

SQL · MySQL

COVID-19 deaths & vaccinations

Problem
How hard did COVID-19 hit each country relative to its cases and population, and how fast did vaccination roll out?
Approach
Loaded Our World in Data CSVs into MySQL, joined the deaths and vaccination tables, and used CTEs, window functions and views.
Outcome
Fatality and infection rates per country, a rolling vaccination count for Bangladesh, and the top 20 countries by confirmed cases.
WITH cte AS (
  SELECT location,
    MAX(CAST(total_deaths AS UNSIGNED)) AS deaths,
    MAX(CAST(total_cases  AS UNSIGNED)) AS cases
  FROM coviddeath
  WHERE continent IS NOT NULL
    AND location = 'bangladesh'
  GROUP BY location)
SELECT location,
  ROUND(deaths / cases * 100, 2) AS fatality_rate
FROM cte;

Power BI · DAX · data model

Sales & customer analysis

Problem
Sales and customer data that was hard to read together across segments, countries, categories and time.
Approach
Cleaned in Power Query, modelled as a star schema, and wrote DAX for month-over-month change, margin and a rank-based customer segmentation. Two pages with year and category slicers.
Outcome
Loyal customers bring in 44.5% of sales. The USA and Germany are the top markets, Drinks and Cheese the top categories, and 2021 peaked before a slight dip in 2022.

Power BI

Three-year sales dashboard

Problem
Three years of sales in separate sheets, with no single view of revenue, cost, margin or salesperson performance.
Approach
Appended the yearly sheets in Power Query, built the data model, and wrote DAX for cumulative sales, margin and profit against target.
Outcome
$25.66M revenue at a 32.5% margin, between 31.7% and 33.3% every quarter. Kids & Toys leads on profit at 43%, and the top seller by revenue was the only top seller to hit the profit target.
